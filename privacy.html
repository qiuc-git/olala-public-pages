Privacy Policy

OLala 1.0.0 · Updated 5 November 2026

Play stays on your phone

OLala 1.0.0 is an offline conversation game. No account is required. We do not run an app backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or recording in this version. Game choices and question changes are not uploaded.

What is saved

Only your Game sounds, Gentle haptics, and Reduce motion preferences are saved on this device. Rounds and feedback notes are held in memory during the current app session. OLala does not save your answers or record conversations. Removing the app removes its local settings, subject to your device backup settings.

Feedback is your choice

If you choose Send email, OLala prepares your note, selected category, app version, build number, and iOS version in an email editor addressed to ligailun18@gmail.com. You review it and choose whether to send. Your email provider processes sent mail under its own policies. A sent email also includes the sender information supplied by your email service. OLala does not send feedback in the background.

External services

Opening an email app or an available App Store listing leaves OLala and uses those services. OLala does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, or location. This policy describes version 1.0.0; future online features will require an updated policy.

Contact and deletion

For privacy questions or to request deletion of feedback held by support, contact ligailun18@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the message; do not send passwords or sensitive documents.
