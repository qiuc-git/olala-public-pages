Privacy Policy

OLala 1.0.0 · Updated 8 November 2026

Scope

OLala is a local, face-to-face conversation game for iOS developed and operated by individual developer ZIPENG XIE ("I" or "me"). This policy explains how information is handled in OLala V1.0.0 and when you contact me for support. Contact: WhatsApp +86 18675971020.

Information during gameplay

You do not need an account to use OLala. The app does not automatically collect personal information and send it to me. It contains no advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not use AI to analyze your conversations.

OLala does not record, save, upload, or analyze your spoken answers. Conversations take place directly between people playing together. Selecting a palm tree, spinning the leaf, drawing a question, or changing a question does not submit your answers to me.

OLala V1.0.0 does not request microphone, camera, photo, contacts, or precise location access for gameplay or feedback. It does not collect advertising identifiers or build user profiles.

Information stored on your device

Your sound, haptics, and reduce-motion preferences are saved on your device. Game state is held in memory during the current app session. The app does not include a feedback form or send feedback to a developer-operated server.

Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in backups. These backups are managed by the device or backup service, not by OLala.

Voluntary feedback and WhatsApp support

WhatsApp support is optional. You can play OLala without contacting me or using WhatsApp. To provide feedback, you may choose to send a message using WhatsApp.

If you choose to contact me via WhatsApp at +86 18675971020, you decide what to share and whether to send a message. Opening a contact link alone does not send a message. If you send a message, I receive its contents and any attachments or information you choose to share. Depending on your settings, WhatsApp may also show me your phone number, profile name, photo, or other profile details.

I use this information to respond to requests, investigate reported issues, provide support, and handle privacy requests. Please do not include sensitive information or other people’s personal information unless necessary. I do not use support messages for advertising or marketing, sell or rent this information, or use it to track you across other apps or websites. WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy policy.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local preferences remain on your device until you remove the app and its data or use any available device data-management options. Separately stored device backups may need to be managed through your backup settings. I cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

I retain support messages for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and resolve requests, or as required by applicable law. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent me, contact me on WhatsApp at +86 18675971020. I may request information reasonably necessary to verify your request. Where applicable law gives you other privacy rights, you may use the same contact method. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it; withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

Children’s privacy

OLala is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for or marketed to children. Store age ratings describe content suitability and are not consent to collect children’s personal information.

The game does not require children or other users to provide personal information to me. Children should not provide their name, contact details, or other personal information in support messages; a parent or guardian should contact me on their behalf.

I do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If I learn that I received information from a child under 13, or below the age at which parental consent is required by applicable law, without required authorization, I will delete it unless the law requires retention. Parents and guardians can contact me via WhatsApp with questions or deletion requests.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the app, information practices, or applicable laws change. The updated policy will be made available in the app and will show its update date. If required by applicable law, I will provide additional notice or obtain consent before relevant changes take effect.
