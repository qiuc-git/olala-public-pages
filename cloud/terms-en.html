OLala Terms — Internal Testing

About this version

OLala is an iOS app for face-to-face conversations with friends you create on your device. Its small activities choose who starts answering a question. This service is currently intended for internal testing. Updated 17 November 2026.

Using your account

Use an Apple or Google account you are authorized to use and provide profile information you are comfortable sharing. Keep access to that provider account secure. You may stop the activity or skip a question at any time.

Your conversations

Spoken conversations take place between the people present. Written reflections and friend progress stay on the current device and do not automatically synchronize to another device. Avoid entering sensitive information about another person without their permission.

Availability and changes

The internal testing service may be interrupted or updated while development and testing continue. Your feedback helps us identify problems. Account and privacy requests can be made through the support contact below.
