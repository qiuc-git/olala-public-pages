Eligibility and services

OLala is an iOS app for people aged 18 or older. Talk offers face-to-face conversation prompts and activities that help choose the first speaker. Word Match is optional and requires an OLala account. Choose a listed room to play with another player or listen to that room.

Your account

Use an account you are authorized to use. Keep your sign-in details secure. Other signed-in adults can enter listed rooms. You can leave a room, end an activity or skip a conversation question at any time.

Community rules

Respect the other player and their privacy. Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual or exploitative content, impersonation, scams, unsolicited promotion, spam and the sharing of private information without permission are prohibited. Do not use OLala to harm anyone or violate applicable laws.

Safety and moderation

Word Match filters disallowed words. If you encounter abusive behavior or content, tap the other player’s avatar and choose Report or Block. You may include a description and an optional image in a report. OLala can investigate reports and restrict room access for violations. Use the Support link below for safety issues or questions about a decision.

Local records and privacy

Face-to-face Talk records stay on your device and do not automatically synchronize. Avoid entering another person’s sensitive information without their permission. The OLala Privacy Policy explains account data, microphone use, reports and deletion.

Changes and contact

We may update the app or its services and will publish material changes to these terms. Contact OLala Support below for questions, account requests or complaints.
