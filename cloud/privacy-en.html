OLala Privacy Notice — Internal Testing

Scope

This notice applies to the OLala internal testing service and its iOS app. Updated 17 November 2026.

Sign-in and account information

OLala uses Apple or Google to verify sign-in. Our account service stores the provider account identifier, your public OLala account ID and the profile information you submit, including nickname, gender, birthday, country and introduction. Session credentials allow you to stay signed in; a profile copy and session credentials are stored on your device.

Photos and cloud providers

A photo you choose is uploaded when you save your profile. Tencent Cloud provides hosting, object storage and image content review. Published profile images may be accessible through their image links. Account data is stored on our server in Hong Kong; other cloud providers process the information required for their services. Apple and Google process sign-in information under their own privacy policies.

Friends, progress and reflections

Friends you create, conversation progress, first-speaker results, elapsed conversation time and written reflections are stored on this device for your signed-in account. They are not uploaded to the account service or synchronized across devices. OLala does not record or upload your spoken answers.

Diagnostics and support

The server records operational information such as request time, route, response status and network address for operation and troubleshooting. Application request logs exclude passwords, identity tokens and message bodies. If you contact us through WhatsApp, we receive the message and information you choose to send; WhatsApp processes that contact under its own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your account profile. Me > Clean Data removes this account’s local friends, conversation progress, timing and reflections on this device. Signing out keeps those local records private to that account. Uninstalling the app does not delete the server account. Device backups are managed through iOS. For access, correction or deletion of server account information, contact the support number below. Test account data remains until removed following a verified request or retirement of this testing environment.
