OLala Privacy Policy

23 November 2026

Scope

This policy covers the OLala iOS app and its online services. OLala is intended for people aged 18 and older.

Account and profile

To use OLala, sign in with Apple, Google or your OLala ID and password. We use provider account identifiers, sign-in verification information, your OLala ID, an app-generated device identifier and session information to authenticate you and protect your account. If you create a password, our server stores a password hash. We store the profile details you provide, including nickname, gender, birthday, country, introduction and any avatar. The app keeps a copy of your profile and session credentials on your device. Your birthday is used to check age eligibility, including for online rooms.

Photos

The iOS photo picker gives OLala the image you select. When you save a new avatar, OLala uploads it to Tencent Cloud storage and checks it for inappropriate content. A published avatar may be accessible through its image link. Selecting a report attachment sends the image only when you submit the report.

Face-to-face Talk

Friends you create, progress, first-speaker selections, timers and written reflections are stored on your device and are not sent to OLala. Talk records are kept separately for each account on the device. Face-to-face Talk does not use the microphone. Your iOS backup settings may affect copies of local records.

Challenge rooms

Signed-in adults can choose a listed voice room. Other people in the room can see your nickname and words revealed in Great Minds. OLala uses account IDs internally to enforce age eligibility, blocks and suspensions. We temporarily process room membership, game state, answers and connection signaling, and keep limited recent participant information so reports can be verified after someone leaves. We use anonymous daily timing totals for game comparisons. If you turn on the microphone from a mic seat, people in the room can hear you live. Audio is sent through WebRTC with encryption in transit. OLala does not record or retain it. Network addresses and connection information establish the connection, including through a Google STUN service and a relay for encrypted traffic when needed.

Safety reports and blocks

When you report a room or a player, OLala stores the reporter, the room or reported account, the reason, the description and any picture you choose to send so authorized moderation staff can investigate. OLala retains reports for 30 days and may keep a record longer while a restriction arising from the report is active. OLala retains blocks with your account to prevent future room contact. Reports do not include automatic voice recordings.

Service providers, feedback and logs

Account data is hosted in Hong Kong. Tencent Cloud provides hosting, storage and avatar content checks. Apple and Google process sign-in data under their own privacy policies. We limit what we share with providers to information needed for their role and require providers acting on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy. If you submit in-app feedback, we use your message, category, language and app and iOS versions to respond. If you contact us on WhatsApp, its policies also apply. Operational logs may include request time, route, status and network information for security and troubleshooting.

Retention, choices and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and keep limited records for security, moderation or legal obligations where necessary. Go to Me → Clean Data to clear Talk records for this account on the device. Go to Me → Settings → Delete account to request deletion of the server account and linked sign-in identities. Following confirmation, OLala signs out account sessions, clears the associated local Talk archive on this device, and processes any remaining account data and avatar cleanup. Copies may persist for a limited time in backups. You can stop microphone access through iOS Settings. For access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion questions, contact OLala Support.
